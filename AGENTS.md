# Dynasty XI Development & Design Guidelines

## 1. Zero Emoji Policy (Strict Enforcement)
- **Never use emojis** anywhere in the codebase, UI, domain entities, logs, notifications, club badges, or commentary.
- **Visuals must use Brutalist Vector & SVG Icons**: All icons, indicators, badges, and meters must be rendered using vector paths, SVG, or dedicated geometric `BrutalistIcons` / `CustomPaint` components.
- **Club Badges**: Clubs use geometric vector crest types (e.g., `SHIELD_HERALDIC`, `EAGLE_MONOGRAM`, `LION_CREST`, `LIGHTNING_BOLT`, `STAR_HEXAGON`, `CROWN_GEOMETRIC`) instead of emoji characters.

## 2. Anti-Slop Tone & Terminology
- Avoid generic AI fluff, hype words, artificial excitement, and exclamation mark spam (e.g., replace "Yıldız Oyuncu!", "Efsane Şov!" with realistic football governance terms like "A Takım Tescili", "Tahkim Dosyası", "Mali Bilanço Raporu").
- Maintain an authentic, technical, and grounded football executive / boardroom tone throughout all player dialogues, press conferences, board interactions, and notifications.
